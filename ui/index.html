Multi-bone weight painting
Select a bone in the tree or below. Paint its desired influence; other bone weights redistribute proportionally to keep the total at 100%. Start from automatic binding; paint 0% to remove influence or 100% to assign it fully.





Blue = 0% · yellow = 50% · red = 100% selected bone influence




Heatmap shows the selected bone on the rest sprite. It refreshes after a brush stroke. Up to 256 stamps per rig. Paint is stored in normalized rest-canvas coordinates. Recheck weights after changing the source image or joint layout. Mesh folds and overlap can still occur.
