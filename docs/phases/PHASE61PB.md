# Phase 61P-B — Advanced slashes and weapon trails

Seven new effect kinds: horizontal, vertical, diagonal, crescent, circular/spin and double slash, plus weapon trail. New tapered ribbons have a growing sweep, bright core, adjustable thickness, trailing echoes, glow and seeded sparks. The existing slash is unchanged. Rotation, speed, radius, opacity, color and active range are inherited from Phase 47. New recipe fields sweep (30–360 degrees), thickness (5–70% of radius), trail and glow (0–100%) round-trip through VFX Studio, validated scene recipes and export. Old recipes receive defaults. The Studio exposes all advanced controls; Scenes can add new presets and retain their fields on ordinary edits. Weapon trails are procedural sweeping ribbons, not sampled from a rig's actual weapon path; bone/weapon attachment is future combat integration. Glow is baked colored pixels, not an additive shader. No AI inference. Existing PNG/GIF packs and current-frame PNG/WebP retained. CPU tests added for short clips, deterministic pixel/smooth playback, transparent cells, validation and backward compatibility; not executed in this environment. Actions remain manual-only; no workflow dispatched. Stacked on 61P-A (#81); desktop acceptance pending. Next 61P-C projectile paths. Packaging on hold.
